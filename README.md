# Genesi

Genesi is a fertility wellness platform built for Zimbabwean women — blending faith, nutrition, lifestyle guidance, and medical awareness into one thoughtful space.

---

## What Genesi Does

### Fertility Check
Answer a few personal questions — your age, how long you've been trying, your BMI, and any conditions you're aware of — and Genesi gives you tailored advice and a meal plan recommendation that fits your situation. It's not one-size-fits-all; it meets you where you are.

### Fertility Log
Track what's happening in your body every day. Log your mood, flow, cervical mucus, symptoms, basal temperature, and more. Over time, Genesi builds a picture of your cycle so you can see patterns, spot your most fertile days, and share meaningful data with your doctor if you need to.

### Ovulation Tracker
A calendar-based view of your cycle. Mark your period days, log temperature and mucus readings, and record ovulation test results. Genesi predicts your next period, your ovulation date, and your fertile window — all visible at a glance.

### Meal Plans
Five meal plans designed around specific fertility concerns, each built with Zimbabwean foods at the centre:

- **General Fertility Boost** — a solid foundation for anyone starting out
- **Ovulation Support** — nutrients that help regulate and support ovulation
- **Hormone Balance** — for those dealing with hormonal irregularities
- **Anti-Inflammatory** — especially helpful for women with fibroids
- **IVF Preparation** — targeted nutrition for the IVF journey

Each plan covers a full week — breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks — so there's no guesswork about what to eat.

### Education
Articles and guidance on fertility, reproductive health, and wellness, written with the African woman's context in mind.

### Your Account
Create a profile to keep your logs private and persistent. You can update your information, review your history, or permanently delete your account and all your data at any time — no hoops to jump through.
